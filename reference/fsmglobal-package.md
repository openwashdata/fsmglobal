# fsmglobal: Global Faecal Sludge Emptying Services Demand

Global faecal sludge emptying demand data for 175 countries and
territories, based on 2017 population figures. Demand is quantified by
the population served by one of four emptying methods (mechanized,
non-mechanized, unemptiable, no facility). The demand is disaggregated
into urban and rural proportions.
